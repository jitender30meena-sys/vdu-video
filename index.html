🖥 Display & floating window

Use fullscreen/landscape while watching. A floating picture-in-picture window is available only in browsers that support it.

▶ Background playback

VDU exposes media-session controls where supported, but a website cannot force Android or Chrome to keep a YouTube video playing after the screen locks. The browser, battery policy and YouTube player decide this.

For reliable screen-off playback, a native Android app with a media foreground service is needed. Installing this website as a PWA does not create that native service.

🛡 Ad blocking & skip controls

VDU's own interface is ad-free and does not load advertising scripts. It cannot block or skip ads inside YouTube's cross-origin player. Use the 10-second skip buttons for video content, not adverts.
